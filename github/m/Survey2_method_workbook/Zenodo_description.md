**Title:**
Data for "Quantum Cognition for Robot Decision-Making: A Systematic Review of Quantum-Like Models for Autonomous Agents"

**Creators:**
- Guru, Yeshwanth — Department of Mechanical Engineering, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Chennai, India — ORCID 0009-0007-6353-4033
- Chauhan, Dev Kunwar Singh — Department of Mechanical Engineering, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Chennai, India — ORCID 0000-0002-1466-4567

**Description:**
Supplementary data for a systematic review of quantum-cognition (quantum-probability) models applied
to autonomous agents. The dataset contains: (1) the data-extraction table for the 67 application
studies, with evidence level (E1–E6), model type, domain, task, method, setup, key result,
limitation, basis of extraction and DOI; (2) the per-study quality appraisal of 61 studies against
seven criteria (met / partly met / not met / not assessable); (3) the classification of 71 theory and
evidence studies; (4) the codebook; and (5) the search strategy. Files are in CSV (UTF-8) and
Markdown. The Python code for the worked examples and the illustrative reinforcement-learning
experiment can be added as a second file (Survey2_AIR_Online_Resource_2_code.py).

**Keywords:** quantum cognition; quantum probability; systematic review; human–robot interaction;
autonomous agents; evidence grading
