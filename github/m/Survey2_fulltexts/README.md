# Survey 2 full texts

Copies of the cited papers found in the repository (`quantum_computing/`, `phd/`, and the Survey 1
full-text folder), renamed by their citation key in `Survey2_AIR_LaTeX_Overleaf/refs.bib`. Every file
was matched by reading its first page, not its file name.

- `primary/`: 17 of the 67 application studies (Table S1).
- `references/`: 57 of the 145 other cited works (theory and evidence studies, reviews, background,
  the 11 E5 exclusions and the method references). `Busemeyer2012.pdf` is a scan without a text layer.
- `index.csv`: every cited work, its group, and the source file it was copied from, or MISSING.
