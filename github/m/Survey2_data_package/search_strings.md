# Search strategy

## Stages 1–3 (corpus of 205 works)

1. **Curated reading list** of 102 items, each checked against publisher, arXiv or repository records
   (about 20 corrected; 3 unlocatable items replaced by 2 verified papers; 2 non-existent titles replaced
   by the real works on the same topic; 2 dropped).
2. **Authors' PDFs**, identified by first-page text rather than file names: 31 works added.
3. **Structured searches** of Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar, arXiv, PubMed and publisher sites, with
   backward and forward citation chasing from Pothos and Busemeyer (2022), Huang et al. (2025),
   Widdows et al. (2021) and Meyer et al. (2022), in two streams:
   - (a) applications of quantum cognition to robots, agents, human–machine teams and reinforcement learning;
   - (b) quantum-cognition theory, critiques, machine learning, large language models and neuroscience, 2016–2026.
   74 works added.

The exact strings and the number of records each search returned were **not logged**. The concept
blocks below reproduce the scope of the two streams; they are a reconstruction, not a log.

```
("quantum cognition" OR "quantum-like" OR "quantum probability" OR "quantum decision theory"
  OR "quantum-inspired" OR "projective simulation")
AND
(robot* OR agent* OR "human-robot" OR autonomous OR "multi-agent" OR swarm
  OR "reinforcement learning" OR "machine learning" OR "language model*" OR trust OR automation)

stream (b): replace the second block by
("order effect*" OR "conjunction fallacy" OR "disjunction effect" OR "sure-thing" OR interference
  OR contextuality OR "Bayesian network*" OR critique)
```
