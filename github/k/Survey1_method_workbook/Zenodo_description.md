**Description:**
Supplementary data for a systematic survey of quantum technologies applied to autonomous robots.
The dataset contains: (1) the data-extraction table for the 72 primary studies, with evidence level
(L1–L6), quantum-execution tag, publication status, robotic task, method, setup, key result,
limitation, and DOI for each study; (2) the per-study quality appraisal against seven criteria
(met / partly met / not met); (3) the codebook defining every column and code; and (4) the
databases and search strings used. Files are in CSV (UTF-8) and Markdown.

**Keywords:** quantum robotics; systematic review; evidence grading; quantum annealing; quantum
reinforcement learning; quantum sensing
